# Establishment

## Purpose

Establishment represents a physical place associated with an organization where attendance may be expected or recorded.

Examples include a school campus, office, gym, store, or company branch.

## Business Context

An organization may have one or several establishments. Depending on its attendance process, a person may be expected to attend a particular establishment or may be permitted to record attendance at more than one location.

An organization may choose to use an establishment as part of attendance validation. This is optional and may not apply to every workflow, such as an attendance process intended for remote or virtual activities.

## Business Purpose

Establishment gives an attendance process a physical-location context. It helps an organization understand where attendance is expected or recorded and supports location validation when that capability is configured.

## Scope

Establishment represents the physical place and its business meaning. It does not represent a person, an attendance record, a schedule, or an organization's opening hours by itself.

Each person's required schedule belongs to that person, not to the establishment. Establishment opening hours describe operating times and must not replace a person's schedule in lateness or schedule-compliance reports.

Whether an attendance process requires a person to be near an establishment depends on the organization's configuration. No single distance or location-validation requirement applies to every organization by default.

## Core Principle

Establishment represents a physical location, not an attendance event. The platform must support location-aware attendance when configured without requiring every organization or workflow to use it.
