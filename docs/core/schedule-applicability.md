# Schedule Applicability

## Purpose

Schedule Applicability describes how a person's required schedule is used to interpret attendance for reporting, separately from any establishment or location context.

## Business Context

Every person must have an associated schedule, regardless of whether they are a teacher, employee, member, or another contextual role. Attendance may also be associated with a specific establishment, but that location context does not determine who owns the schedule.

Opening hours and a person's schedule are not the same thing. Opening hours describe when an establishment is operating; the person's schedule describes when that individual is expected to attend. Opening hours do not replace the person's schedule.

## Business Purpose

Schedule Applicability keeps all lateness and schedule-compliance reports grounded in the person's applicable schedule and configured event meanings, without confusing expected attendance with establishment opening hours.

## Scope

The relevant contexts have distinct responsibilities:

- **Person's schedule:** required for every person and the reference for lateness and schedule-compliance reports.
- **Establishment:** the physical-location context of attendance when applicable.
- **Opening hours:** the establishment's operating times, not a substitute for an individual's schedule.

This concept does not define calculation formulas, schedule assignment, schedule selection when periods overlap, or location-validation rules. Those details remain to be specified. A schedule mismatch alone does not block an otherwise successful capture.

## Core Principle

Every person must have a schedule associated with them, not with the establishment. All lateness and schedule-compliance reporting uses that person's applicable schedule; location context and opening hours do not replace it.
