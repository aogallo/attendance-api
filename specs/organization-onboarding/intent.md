# Organization Onboarding — Intent

**Status:** Original intent and clarification context, not a separate living rulebook or an implementation specification.

The confirmed behavior is now consolidated in the [proposed behavior specification](spec.md), with a [proposed implementation and test plan](implementation-plan.md). Both await owner review; they do not authorize application implementation or remote configuration. This intent remains original context, not an independent copy to update. For current confirmed requirements, follow [product decisions](../../docs/product-decisions.md), [administrative access policy](../../docs/security/administrative-access.md), and [email delivery policy](../../docs/operations/email-delivery.md). Older general pending-work lists below do not reopen specifically approved policies. Once the spec is accepted, maintain feature behavior there; keep this context for rationale rather than copying new rules into it.

## Objective

Allow a Platform Administrator to create and configure an organization and invite its Organization Administrator, so the organization can continue its initial setup and begin using the attendance platform.

## Business outcome

A new organization can be established in the platform with its attendance-related configuration and designated administrators. Each administrator receives organization-scoped responsibilities after accepting a valid invitation. Multiple administrators are supported from the first release, with no product-defined maximum number per organization.

Organization creation is independently supported: the organization exists before an invitation can associate an administrator with it. An invitation may be issued later rather than as part of creation. The organization can therefore exist without an issued or accepted invitation; existence alone does not imply readiness for attendance operations.

## Actors

- **Platform Administrator:** creates and configures an organization and issues the Organization Administrator invitation.
- **Organization Administrator / invitee:** accepts a valid invitation and gains administration access limited to that organization.

## Confirmed product behavior

- Password-recovery requests, including manual requests for a new recovery link, are processed at least 60 seconds apart per submitted email address, with a maximum of 5 processed requests in a rolling one-hour window. Additionally, process at most 30 requests per IP address in a rolling one-hour window, summed across all submitted email addresses, and at most 20 requests globally per UTC calendar day, summed across all email addresses and IP addresses. The global window resets at midnight UTC, not after a rolling 24 hours; all three limit layers must be satisfied. Apply the same limits and generic response to existing and nonexistent accounts. Reaching any limit prevents additional recovery processing/email sending for the limited requests, without blocking accounts or sign-in, or sending additional emails. The IP threshold is an initial value to validate against real usage, accounting for schools and companies sharing a public IP. The global budget is an initial Resend Free planning value, not guaranteed capacity or a reservation of the remaining provider quota. These limits do not define sign-in, invitation-resend, or email-verification limits; allocation across email flows, trusted client-IP determination, counting/failure semantics, and technical enforcement remain pending. No live configuration or sending is authorized here.
- When Resend's daily or monthly quota is exhausted, do not promise delivery or automatically retry rejected sends. Record the failure and show it to the Platform Administrator without depending on another email. Preserve created organizations and invitations without extending expiry, and maintain the generic password-recovery response without revealing account existence. When capacity returns, allow explicit manual retry under the existing eligibility and rate-limit rules; an expired invitation requires explicit replacement and an expired authentication link requires a manual new-link request. Do not send expired links later, purchase services, or upgrade the plan automatically. Detection, operational visibility, and enforcement remain implementation-design work under TD-007.
- Password-recovery requests must not reveal whether an account exists for the submitted email. Existing and nonexistent accounts receive the same user-facing response: “Si existe una cuenta asociada a ese correo, recibirás instrucciones para recuperar tu contraseña”. This includes manual requests for a new recovery link, does not assert successful delivery, and does not replace the separate notice for an expired link. HTTP response details, timing, provider-error handling, rate limits, and abuse protections remain to be designed and tested; this is not an implemented security control.
- Email-verification and password-recovery links are valid for 30 minutes for both administrative roles. Their intended shared Supabase Email OTP Expiration setting is 1800 seconds, not an already-applied configuration. Application-owned organization invitations retain their separate 15-minute validity from record creation; verification or recovery does not extend it or waive MFA. When an authentication link has expired, clearly inform the person and allow an explicit manual request for a new link; do not automatically send another link upon expiry or opening the expired link. Requesting or sending a new authentication link does not extend, restart, replace, or reactivate an organization invitation; an expired invitation still requires explicit replacement by a Platform Administrator. Remaining message copy, rate-limit and abuse-protection enforcement, and provider integration remain pending; no real email delivery is authorized here.
- Successful issuance of a new verification or recovery link invalidates earlier links for the same account and same purpose. The new link has 30 minutes of validity from successful issuance; other accounts, purposes, and organization invitations are unaffected. A mere request, rate-limit rejection, or failed issuance is not a successful replacement: an earlier link retains only its original eligibility and expiry, without extension or reactivation. Issuance is not proof of delivery; the provider-backed success condition and invalidation enforcement remain to be designed and verified.
- Initial organization-invitation, email-verification, and password-recovery emails use neutral, professional Spanish. Exact templates, content, and delivery configuration remain pending; the language decision does not authorize real email delivery or select a localization mechanism.
- Both administrative roles use passwords of at least 15 characters, allowing long passphrases without mandatory character-type mixtures. Do not require periodic changes; require a change upon evidence of password compromise. Permit password managers, autofill, and pasting passwords. Reject common or known-compromised passwords; the blocking mechanism, detailed enforcement, and cost remain pending, without authorization to purchase a plan or service.
- Explicit administrator actions reset the 30-minute inactivity timer: navigating between sections, manually querying information, or saving changes. Moving the mouse, leaving a tab open, receiving automatic updates, automatic token renewals, and background queries do not reset it. These actions do not reset or extend the 8-hour maximum session duration and cannot revive an expired session without fresh email/password authentication and successful TOTP MFA. Trusted activity tracking remains to be designed.
- For either administrative role, ordinary sign-out invalidates only the current session; other sessions of the same account, including those on other devices, remain unaffected. Authorized lost-authenticator recovery still invalidates all existing sessions of the affected account. Administrative sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or a maximum duration of 8 hours, whichever occurs first. After either limit, fresh email/password authentication and successful TOTP MFA are required; token refresh alone must not bypass these limits. Trusted activity tracking, renewal mechanics, and technical enforcement remain pending; this does not define the lifecycle of capture-only attendance sessions.
- An account may sign in with email and password to configure and verify TOTP before completing MFA. Administrative operations for either role remain blocked until MFA is successfully completed; configuring a factor alone is insufficient. MFA completion does not grant roles or accept an invitation. Verified invited email and successfully completed TOTP MFA are prerequisites for completing invitation acceptance. Email verification and MFA setup/completion do not extend the invitation's original 15-minute validity period from record creation or bypass approved lost-factor recovery.
- Every recovery of either administrative role must have an audit record identifying who authorized it, the affected account, the date/time, and the outcome, without recording passwords, tokens, or TOTP secrets. Audit storage, access, retention, and failure handling remain to be designed.
- The platform supports organizations from different business contexts; onboarding must not hardcode school-, company-, or gym-specific behavior into the shared core.
- Organization configuration includes enabled attendance methods, the person-registration mode, and whether location validation is enabled.
- An organization may enable multiple attendance methods. A person uses one enabled method for a given capture. Enabled methods are available across the organization's establishments by default.
- Facial recognition is the first method to be delivered and the product's primary attendance method. It is the only attendance method in the first attendance release. PIN and fingerprint recognition are deferred to later releases and are not available methods or fallbacks in that first release. This does not automatically enable facial recognition for the organization or bring biometric enrollment or capture implementation into Organization Onboarding.
- Each organization selects exactly one person-registration mode: administrator-managed registration or self-registration.
- Location validation is optional. When location is unavailable during attendance capture, attendance can still be recorded; the result must not claim location was successfully validated.
- The Platform Administrator invites the Organization Administrator.
- Both administrative roles require email/password authentication plus TOTP MFA from an authenticator application from the first delivery. The API must reject password-only access to Platform Administrator and Organization Administrator operations; invitation acceptance and password recovery do not waive MFA. The initial Platform Administrator is established before operating onboarding through the restricted owner-invoked bootstrap in [TD-005](../../docs/technical-decisions/005-initial-platform-administrator-bootstrap.md), after account verification and MFA completion. Detailed enrollment/bootstrap controls, recovery authority/evidence/session controls, and reauthentication recency require further definition. Ordinary Person captures through attendance mode do not require individual accounts or TOTP challenges.
- Recovery after loss of either administrator's authenticator is manual with identity verification. An authorized recovery invalidates the affected account's previous authentication factors and existing sessions. A new TOTP factor must be enrolled and successfully verified before restoring administrative functions, without granting new roles or an MFA exemption. Only a Platform Administrator with valid MFA-authenticated access may authorize Organization Administrator recovery, after verifying that account holder's identity; Organization Administrators cannot authorize their own or another administrator's recovery. Platform Administrator recovery is reserved to the platform owner/operator through a separate restricted operational procedure, with identity verification and a successfully verified new TOTP factor before access returns. Acceptable evidence, restricted procedure authorization/execution, technical enforcement of factor/session invalidation, and audit controls remain to be approved; this is not password recovery, public self-service recovery, or initial bootstrap reuse.
- The Platform Administrator must first create the organization. Creating it does not require immediately issuing an administrator invitation; invitations may be issued later for that existing organization. Administrative authority still requires successful invitation acceptance.
- The required business information at organization creation is the organization's name and the email address of the intended initial Organization Administrator. That email is required even if the account does not exist and the invitation is deferred. Saving it does not create the account, grant permissions, create attendance-related records, or automatically issue an invitation. Invitation validity begins only when the invitation record is created.
- Attendance-profile setup may follow organization creation. Enabled methods, person-registration mode, and the location-validation choice are not required at creation. Before recording attendance, at least one supported method must be enabled, exactly one registration mode selected, and location validation explicitly enabled or disabled; incomplete setup must not be silently filled with unapproved defaults. Location validation itself remains optional.
- Before invitation acceptance, the Platform Administrator may edit the created organization's business information and attendance configuration, including enabled methods, person-registration mode, and optional location validation. These edits do not change an existing invitation's recipient email or restart its validity period. Additional organization fields and permissions after acceptance remain to be defined.
- An organization may have multiple Organization Administrators from the first release, with no product-defined maximum number. Each administrator must accept a valid invitation and receives authority limited to the intended organization.
- Each invitation is bound to the intended organization and a specific recipient email address. Only the identity corresponding to that email may accept it; an identity with a different email cannot gain administrative access by receiving a forwarded link.
- Completing acceptance requires authentication with the account corresponding to the invited email, provider verification of that email, and successfully completed TOTP MFA. If no account exists, it may be created within the same flow, followed by satisfying those requirements before completing acceptance. Opening the link alone, a matching but unverified email, a password-only session, or authentication without an eligible invitation does not grant administrative authority. If the invitation expires during email verification or MFA setup/completion, reject acceptance without granting the role; a Platform Administrator must explicitly issue a replacement.
- [TD-004](../../docs/technical-decisions/004-initial-administrative-authentication.md) approves Supabase Auth with email/password sign-in, mandatory TOTP MFA for both administrative roles, and password recovery through a link sent to the account email. Email confirmation and password recovery are separate flows: they do not consume an invitation, grant organization permissions, restart its 15-minute validity period, or reactivate it. The invitation must still be eligible when acceptance completes. Password policy, detailed confirmation/recovery handling, MFA enrollment, detailed lost-factor recovery controls, and session details remain to be defined.
- Invitation acceptance supports both account cases: reuse the existing account for the invited email, or allow creation of an account during acceptance if none exists for that email. Reusing an account must not require a duplicate account. Neither case automatically creates a Person record, schedule, or attendance-method enrollment. The provider and initial access flow are approved in TD-004; detailed account integration remains to be designed.
- The Platform Administrator has no attendance or schedule. An Organization Administrator may only administer without a Person record, attendance enrollment, or schedule, or also participate in attendance through an associated Person within the organization. Attendance participation requires a personal schedule and the same attendance and reporting rules as other people; it does not require a second login account merely because the individual also administers.
- Accepting an administrator invitation grants administrative authority only. It does not automatically create a Person record, assign a schedule, or enroll the administrator in attendance. Person registration and attendance enrollment remain subsequent responsibilities outside this feature.
- The invitation is valid for 15 minutes from the creation of its record, not from delivery or opening the link. The Platform Administrator must explicitly issue a replacement; replacement is not automatic.
- Successful acceptance permanently consumes the invitation; another acceptance is rejected even within the original validity period. Expired, replaced, or explicitly revoked invitations cannot be accepted.
- Issuing a new invitation for the same recipient email and the same organization replaces all earlier pending Organization Administrator invitations for that combination and makes them unusable. Invitations for other emails or organizations are unaffected, including the same email in another organization. Completed acceptances are not undone. Retaining or deleting replaced invitation records remains a technical decision.
- Only the Platform Administrator may explicitly revoke an Organization Administrator invitation by changing its state. Organization Administrators cannot revoke these invitations.
- Acceptance must be rejected if replacement of that invitation has already begun. Acceptance completed before replacement began must not be silently undone. The technical ordering mechanism remains undecided.
- If replacement begins but fails before the new invitation record is created, the prior invitation remains unusable. The failure is clearly shown to the Platform Administrator, who may explicitly retry; the prior invitation must not be automatically reactivated, and the failed creation must not be presented as a newly created invitation.
- If delivery fails after an initial or replacement invitation record was successfully created, preserve the organization and invitation and clearly show the failure to the Platform Administrator. That administrator may manually resend the same invitation while it remains eligible for acceptance, without restarting its original 15-minute period or reactivating an unusable invitation. An expired invitation requires an explicit replacement instead. [TD-007](../../docs/technical-decisions/007-transactional-email-delivery.md) approves Resend with the sending subdomain `notificaciones.allangallo.dev` and sender `Attendance <no-reply@notificaciones.allangallo.dev>`: custom SMTP for Supabase Auth verification/password-recovery messages and a separate API integration for application-owned invitations. Domain verification, sender configuration, contracting, and detailed integration remain pending; configuration is not authorized and the application's invitation lifecycle is not replaced by provider authentication invitations.
- The Organization Administrator manages establishments for their own organization. At least one establishment must exist before a person can enroll. Establishment creation is a subsequent organization-setup responsibility, not automatically part of creating the organization.
- Data and administrative access must be isolated by organization.
- API v2 is a new implementation in this repository. An existing client currently uses API v1 and is expected to migrate at a later time. The v1 code/client are not present in this repository unless supplied separately; do not assume its implementation details. The migration scope and technical approach are not yet decided.

## Proposed first-release scope

- Require the organization's name and the intended initial administrator's email to create an organization. Reject creation when either is missing, without requiring that administrator's account to already exist or an invitation to be issued immediately.
- Require a TOTP MFA-authenticated Platform Administrator session for platform operations such as organization creation/configuration and invitation issuance/replacement/revocation; reject password-only access without performing the requested operation.
- Require Organization Administrators to satisfy TOTP MFA before exercising their organization-scoped administrative permissions; invitation acceptance must not be treated as an exemption, and password-only sessions must not perform those operations.
- Allow organization creation without issuing an administrator invitation, then allow a later invitation associated with that existing organization. An invitation must not refer to an organization that does not yet exist.
- Allow the organization to be created before attendance-profile setup is completed, and allow configuration during subsequent setup. Require completion before recording attendance, without inventing default settings.
- Allow the Platform Administrator to edit organization business information and attendance configuration after creation and before invitation acceptance, without changing the recipient email or validity period of an existing invitation.
- Select one person-registration mode.
- Support configuring facial recognition as the only available method for the first attendance release. Do not offer PIN or fingerprint recognition as available methods or fallbacks in that release. This configuration may be deferred until after organization creation, but facial recognition must be enabled before recording attendance.
- Enable or disable location validation.
- Issue an invitation for the Organization Administrator.
- Support multiple Organization Administrators per organization without a product-defined maximum number, applying the invitation and acceptance rules to each administrator.
- Bind the invitation to the intended organization and recipient email. Reject acceptance by an identity with a different email without granting administrative authority.
- Support invitation acceptance using the existing account for the invited email, or account creation during acceptance when no account exists for that email, without automatically creating attendance-related records.
- Require the invitee to be authenticated with the account corresponding to the invited email before completing acceptance. Do not grant the administrative role to an unauthenticated visitor, an account with another email, or an authenticated account whose invitation does not satisfy the acceptance rules.
- Require the invited email to be verified by the authentication provider and TOTP MFA successfully completed before completing acceptance. Reject a password-only session without consuming the invitation or granting the role. Support email/password sign-in and email-link password recovery without granting organization permissions or extending invitation validity; email verification and MFA setup/completion also do not restart or extend the original 15-minute period.
- Accept an invitation only while it is within its 15-minute validity period, has not been consumed, replaced, or revoked, and its replacement has not begun.
- Reject an expired invitation and require the Platform Administrator to issue a replacement manually.
- Reject repeated acceptance of an already consumed invitation and acceptance of replaced or revoked invitations.
- When issuing a new invitation, invalidate all earlier pending invitations for the same recipient email and organization, without affecting other emails, other organizations, or completed acceptances.
- Allow explicit invitation revocation only by the Platform Administrator; reject revocation by an Organization Administrator.
- Reject acceptance if replacement has already begun, without silently undoing an acceptance completed before replacement began.
- If creation of a replacement invitation fails after replacement begins, keep the prior invitation unusable, clearly report the failure to the Platform Administrator, and allow an explicit retry without claiming that a new invitation was created.
- If delivery fails after successful invitation creation, preserve the organization and invitation, clearly report the delivery failure, and allow the Platform Administrator to manually resend the same eligible invitation without extending its expiry. Require an explicit replacement after expiry.
- Ensure the resulting Organization Administrator has access only to the associated organization.
- Allow an administration-only Organization Administrator to accept the invitation without a Person record, attendance-method enrollment, or schedule. Creating these attendance-related records is not part of invitation acceptance.

## Out of scope for this feature

- Creating establishments, people, schedules, or attendance events.
- Granting the initial Platform Administrator role through public registration, organization creation, or a migration containing a real privileged account. Initial platform setup follows TD-005 separately and must already be complete before operating the organization's onboarding flow.
- Enrolling PIN, fingerprint, or facial references.
- Building attendance capture, reporting, membership, payroll, or access-control workflows.
- Implementing shared-device attendance mode, its capture-session lifecycle and administrator reauthentication, or organization-specific permanent device registration extensions. Initial attendance uses shared devices without individual Person logins; this does not add those capture capabilities to Organization Onboarding.
- Delivering customer-operated installations or integrating a buyer's enterprise identity provider. The initial delivery is hosted by the project owner; the future commercial scenario is retained for later evaluation in [TD-003](../../docs/technical-decisions/003-delivery-model-and-customer-identity.md), not added to this feature's scope. [TD-004](../../docs/technical-decisions/004-initial-administrative-authentication.md) selects the initial platform's administrative provider without adding customer identity-provider integrations.
- Migrating the existing client from API v1 to API v2.
- Implementing the API v1-to-v2 client migration.
- Defining endpoints, database tables, classes, or code structure. These belong to technical planning after the owner approves the initial technical foundation and the product behavior is clarified.

## Remaining specification and technical work

The previously listed onboarding product questions have been resolved in the confirmed behavior above. This document is still an intent, not a final behavior specification or authorization to implement. Consolidate the confirmed flows into testable acceptance criteria and surface any newly discovered product gaps rather than inventing answers. The approved foundation and application/API-contract conventions are recorded in [TD-001](../../docs/technical-decisions/001-api-foundation.md) and [TD-002](../../docs/technical-decisions/002-application-structure-and-api-contract.md), with the administrative provider and initial access flow selected in [TD-004](../../docs/technical-decisions/004-initial-administrative-authentication.md) and initial platform-role bootstrap defined in [TD-005](../../docs/technical-decisions/005-initial-platform-administrator-bootstrap.md). Password policy, detailed confirmation/recovery handling, MFA enrollment, detailed lost-factor recovery controls, sessions, detailed bootstrap safeguards, invitation ordering enforcement, email delivery, and detailed persistence/transaction design still require decisions.

## Acceptance direction (not final criteria)

The feature should be considered specified when its creation, configuration, invitation, acceptance, expiry, replacement, authorization scope, error outcomes, and out-of-scope behavior are unambiguous enough to derive acceptance criteria without inventing business rules.
